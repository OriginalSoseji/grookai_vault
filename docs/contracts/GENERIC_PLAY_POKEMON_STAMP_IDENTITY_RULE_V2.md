# Generic Play! Pokémon stamp identity rule V2

Status: bounded evidence preparation under qualification; no production admission.

This separately versioned model extends the original single-series evidence
scope without changing historical V1 sources or the active stamp admission stop.
It follows PRINTED_IDENTITY_MODEL_V1, STAMPED_IDENTITY_RULE_V1,
VARIANT_COEXISTENCE_RULE_V1 and PRINTING_TRUTH_CONTRACT_V1.

The identity is the unique canonical underlying base plus the observed generic
Play! stamp: `variant_key = play_pokemon_stamp`. One or several documented Prize
Pack series are distribution provenance. They do not create separate canonical
parents when the card does not print a series marker. A printed series marker,
another specific stamp, ambiguous base, unknown alias or existing generic stamped
parent requires separate review. Never derive the stamp from a provider slug,
price, foil bucket, checklist membership alone or shared unstamped artwork.

This implementation is restricted to nine frozen source targets. Its original
official checklists, independent extracted checklist, nine individually reviewed
card-front images and literal visual observations are byte-bound. It preserves
three confirmed repeated coordinates without claiming complete series membership.
Retain the literal Series Seven SHF/SFA coordinate conflicts and Series Six
Pecharunt ex/Genesect SFA040 conflict. Do not silently fix source tokens or admit
any ambiguous foil identity. Normal checklist material is retained as evidence;
this identity model grants no printing or finish authority.

Five approved regular-base aliases exposed four already-existing stamped Normal
parents. Those four are excluded and must never be created again. The ninth
missing target, Night Stretcher, uses its approved canonical base alias while
preserving both legacy and canonical IDs and their original number formatting.
Do not infer finishes across any base or stamped alias. Keep GameStop and World
Championship deck variants as distinct identities.

The pure offline adjudicator requires exact current base, one active base
identity, approved base-alias records, literal image observations and explicit
current collision input. Changed evidence fails closed. A lawful result states
NEW_CANONICAL_REQUIRED and CREATE_CARD_PRINT, with card_printings action NONE.
It is a review result, not approval, live uniqueness proof, warehouse staging,
canonical execution or public-ID reservation. Public IDs must be minted through
the governed warehouse assignment path; do not append a guessed zero-padded
suffix to a legacy base ID.

Before any execution, qualify the warehouse bridge/classifier/staging and bounded
executor, actual public-ID shape, complete current global/base-alias collision
scope, retained owner and public references, rollback and independent readback.
Require the ordinary exact owner decision for the concrete plan and full source.
No direct canonical writer or automatic founder approval belongs here.

All 32 held targets, old 98 inserts and 32 links remain stopped. This model has
no clearance flag, alias/quarantine/reparent operation, price publication or
owner mutation. The original149 completed inserts must never be repeated.
Four older Prize97 Normal children and every historical review remain unchanged.
