# MEE Pricing Platform V1.1 Parking Lot

**Status: PARKED**

This file prevents valid future ideas from entering the frozen Production V1
release branch. Inclusion here is not approval, prioritization, or a design
decision.

## Parked Scope

- Japanese pricing
- additional non-English pricing
- slabs and graded-card pricing
- sealed products
- special-variant expansion
- Grookai Value
- sold-history weighting
- proprietary valuation models
- additional market sources as headline authorities
- other TCGs

## Intake Rule

New pricing ideas are recorded here unless they directly repair a contradiction
with the frozen Production V1 Definition of Done.

No parked item may be implemented, migrated, deployed, or used to delay
Production V1.

Each item requires a separately versioned contract, evidence plan, and rollout
decision before leaving the parking lot.
