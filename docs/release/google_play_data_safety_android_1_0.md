# Google Play Data Safety: Android 1.0

This worksheet mirrors the shipping Android application and the public privacy policy. It is a release input, not proof that the answers have been submitted in Play Console.

## High-Level Answers

- Does the app collect or share required user data types: Yes, it collects data needed for app functionality.
- Is all user data encrypted in transit: Yes.
- Can users request deletion: Yes, at `https://grookaivault.com/account/delete`.
- Does the app contain ads: No.
- Is data used for advertising or cross-app tracking: No.
- Is collection optional: User content and camera/photo uploads are optional; account and operational identifiers are required for signed-in functionality.

## Data Collected

Personal info:

- Name: optional profile identity; app functionality.
- Email address: authentication, account support, and account recovery; app functionality and account management.
- User IDs: account ownership and access control; app functionality and fraud prevention.

Photos and videos:

- Card photos, profile media, and other images selected by the user; app functionality.

Messages:

- In-app collector messages; app functionality.

App activity:

- Product interactions such as vault actions, follows, and feature use; analytics and app functionality.

App info and performance:

- Crash logs and diagnostics; analytics, security, and app functionality.

Device or other IDs:

- Notification registration and installation identifiers; app functionality and fraud prevention.

## Data Sharing

Grookai Vault does not sell user data and does not share collected data for advertising or tracking. Service providers process data to provide authentication, hosting, storage, diagnostics, notifications, and distribution. Public profile, Wall, and memory content is shared with other users only when the collector enables the applicable public setting.

## Account Deletion

The public deletion page explains how to request full or partial deletion, identity verification, the 30-day processing target, and limited legal/security retention exceptions.

## Console-Only Work

The Play Console questionnaire, target audience declaration, content rating questionnaire, app-access credentials, and final release declarations must be completed in the authenticated developer account. Credentials must remain outside the repository.
