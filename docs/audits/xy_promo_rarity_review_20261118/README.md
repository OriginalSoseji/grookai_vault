# XY promo rarity record-specific review

This package reviews the canonical rarity of exactly two records: XY177 Karen and XY198 M Camerupt-EX. Their prior parent rarity was `Common`. The retained TCGdex payload and `pokemon:stats` trait say `Common`; the exact mapped Pokémon API payload and TCGplayer product say `Promo`. These differing source claims are preserved, including the TCGdex traits. The JSON snapshots represent database JSONB readback, not original HTTP bytes.

The decision for these two records is `Promo`. Their catalog card images visibly show the printed PROMO star and matching numbers; the mapped TCGplayer product images show corresponding designs. The image receipts and exact image bytes are retained in `catalog_images/` and `product_images/`. These are images of card designs, not an inspection of physical copies, and do not establish a finish. The two Bulbapedia articles in `checklists/` corroborate the XY Black Star Promo family and distinguish the numbered `a` variants; the Karen article combines artwork for variants and is not used alone to prove the exact image. Sources may share upstream data, so their claims are not assumed independent.

The policy binds each record to its UUID, name, number, active identity, exact source mappings, product and group, raw source payloads, image bytes, and a completed source sync. It makes no general `Common` to `Promo` source-precedence rule. The earlier rarity review admitted only its explicitly enumerated phrase-order differences and left other disagreements for review; this is a separate, record-specific adjudication.

`xy-rarity-review-candidates.json` and `xy-rarity-visual-review.json` preserve earlier non-executable review stages. `xy-rarity-execution-plan.json` is the separately frozen two-row guarded package. The writer requires the same-writer rollback proof, checks current source and image bytes, stages durable source snapshots, and changes only the two exact parent rarity fields in a serializable transaction. It verifies protected state, source data, and durable receipts. Run reports record the actual outcome; the existence of this package alone does not imply that the update was applied.

The broader read-only inventory found five XY promo records with a Pokémon API `Promo` versus a different parent rarity: XY150, XY177, XY198, XY200a, and XY67a. XY150 and the suffix variants remain outside this policy. The combined earlier inventories contained 147 distinct leads after excluding the already repaired McDonald's records; these are leads, not a complete census or confirmed errors.
